# Review of gel-demo-v1

Checked from frames across 0–58 s and the audio track.

## Issues

- **No product on screen (10–52 s).** 42 of 60 seconds are striped placeholder boxes. The video fails "a working product, demonstrated."
- **Stills held 8–14 s.** Even with real captures, a 3% zoom on a still is dead air. These moments need screen recordings.
- **Guessed callouts.** At 30 s the "Cited passage" box sits on the slot text. On real captures the boxes would miss their targets.
- **Badge off the frame (20–23 s).** The "Local · Qwen3 4B" pill hangs over the frame's bottom-right edge.
- **Top-heavy hook (0–5 s).** Everything sits in the top 40%. The flying IDs overshoot the cloud as garbled text and leave an empty card.
- **Offline claimed, not shown (8–10 s).** "Wi-Fi off" is a drawn icon, not the real menu bar.
- **Voice promised, never shown (10–14 s).** "Ask by voice or by typing" sits over a typed question.
- **Empty effects.** The redact sweep line passes over a still. "Copy a record" is captioned but the copy never appears.
- **Crammed local stack (52–56 s).** Five pills, a number and a footnote share 4 s. The footnote is visible for about 1.3 s.
- **Monotone motion.** Every scene uses the same rise-in. Captions overlap frame edges.
- **Silent.** No narration, no sound.

## Content gaps

- **No target user.** Name HR and payroll teams at Philippine SMEs and BPOs, under the Data Privacy Act.
- **No explicit "why local".** The submission must answer it; the video should say it once.
- **No offline proof or voice input.** These are the two strongest Local AI moments.
- **No B2C beat.** The Personal pack could take 3 seconds if time allows.

## Recommendations

1. Replace every still with a short recording of the real app. Place callouts afterwards, at measured positions.
2. Add the narration and burn in synced subtitles, so the video still works muted on X.
3. Record Wi-Fi turning off in the real menu bar and the spoken question.
4. Rebuild the hook full-frame and give the local stack about 6 s.
5. Vary transitions: push between app beats, a match cut from chip to viewer, a fade only into the end card.
