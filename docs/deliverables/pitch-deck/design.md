# S8 · Pitch deck — Design

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080.
- **Colours** (from `docs/features/app-shell/design.md`): canvas `#F7F5F0`, text `#1F1D1A`, secondary `#4F4A42`, accent `#1F7A4D`, deep accent `#0F4A2D` for big numbers, danger `#B3402E` only for "leak" moments.
- **Type:** a clean sans (Inter, or Canva's closest to SF Pro). Headline 64–80 pt semibold; big numbers 200+ pt; body 32–40 pt; source lines 18–20 pt in secondary colour, bottom-left.
- **Mark:** the Gel drop (teardrop, gradient `#4FC48A → #1F7A4D`) on slides 1 and 8; small in the corner elsewhere.
- **Layout:** generous margins (8% each side), one idea per slide, left-aligned except slides 1, 4 and 8 (centred).
- **Stat slides (2, 3):** the number fills the left two-thirds; the one-line meaning beside it; the source line under it. No icons, no stock photos of hackers or padlocks.
- **Diagram (6):** three rounded boxes left to right inside a "Your Mac" frame; a dotted line out to "Cloud (optional)" passing through a "Redactor" box; a placeholder chip `[SSS_1]` on the line.
- **No animations** except simple appear on slide 6's dotted path (or none, if exporting to PDF).
- **Screenshots:** none required; if used, only real app screenshots with synthetic data.
