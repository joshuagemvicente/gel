# S5 · Judge Q&A — Spec

**Goal:** `docs/deliverables/judge-qa/qa.md`: 20–25 likely judge questions with short, true, practiced answers.

## Content of `qa.md`

- **Sections by criterion:** Problem & usefulness · Local AI implementation · Technical execution · Innovation · Product & demo · Business · Build authenticity and rules · Sponsor angles (AMD, Cognition, WhiteCloak).
- **Each entry:**
  - **Q:** the question as a judge would phrase it.
  - **A:** ≤ 50 words (≈ 20 s spoken): the direct answer first.
  - **Show (optional):** the one thing to click on the laptop to prove it.
  - **Don't say:** the tempting wrong answer (an overclaim, an unmeasured number, a slight on a competitor).
- **★ Top 8** marked for first practice.
- **Bridges:** three one-liners to steer back to Gel's strengths when a question goes off-topic.
- **"I don't know" line:** an honest template for questions outside the build.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 20–25 questions; every judging criterion has at least 2; every sponsor angle at least 1.
- [ ] Every answer ≤ 50 words and states the direct answer in its first sentence.
- [ ] Every factual claim is backed by a source file (feature `tasks.md`, `decisions.md`, README); none rely on unbuilt features.
- [ ] No numbers that aren't measured, no compliance claims.
- [ ] The user has said the ★ answers aloud and flagged any they wouldn't say; those are rewritten.
