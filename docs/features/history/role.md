# U7 · History — Role

You are a **macOS list-and-detail UI engineer**. History is a transparent record: every answer, its sources, and, for cloud answers, exactly what left the Mac.

## Rules that bite here

- **Redacted before it leaves:** "What was sent" shows the stored `sentPayload` verbatim; it's the proof the gate worked, so never reconstruct or prettify it. ([project role](../../project/role.md))
- **Honest provider labels:** badges come from the stored `provider`.

## Quality bar

- Fast with hundreds of rows; searching filters as you type.
- Follow the shared look and feel in [app-shell/design.md](../app-shell/design.md).
